# S0-S1 figure captions and visual inspection

All figures come from saved 4 kHz samples; physical-field calculations in this run: zero. Axes use x in meters and z positive downward. PDF exports are vector; PNGs are fixed-size 2188 x 1240 pixels.

- **F01** Geometry: Tx at (0,100) m, nine fixed receivers centered at (0,3) m, strong sinusoidal physical surface z=-eta, C2 plateau |x|<=42 m and support |x|<=50 m. The E02 saved x-z sampling window is 0.5:0.125:6 m. The source-to-receiver panel and surface panel use different scales, visibly labeled. Inspection: all nine point IDs present, downward z direction explicit, no false full-domain field claim.
- **F03** Strong-sine raw reflected field: 411 x 45 actual saved complex samples per method. Top row is magnitude, bottom row native principal phase. PE/BIE share one magnitude ceiling; Bellhop has its own native source scale. Inspection: three columns, common PE/BIE scale, separate Bellhop scale and common [-pi,pi] phase range present; point overlay at the center is visible. Similar large-scale magnitude envelopes are not evidence of small complex error.
- **F04** Three-sine z=3 m G line: full and local magnitude/phase, original M99 support, marks at P21/P22/P23 only. Inspection: all three cases and both spatial scales present; phase-wrap gaps do not join branches. P12/P32 are correctly absent from this depth line.
- **F05** PE-BIE G line residual: complex absolute difference, signed magnitude-ratio dB and wrapped phase. The residual axes disclose their own scales. Inspection: phase/dB axes labeled separately; no normalization or smoothing is applied.
- **F05_grid** Strong-sine 3 x 3 G residual: only middle-row cells P21/P22/P23 have saved G. Gray top and bottom cells are missing, not zero or interpolated. The strongest saved relative error is P21, 0.0781851891; complete nine-point worst-point claims remain unavailable. Inspection: six missing cells visibly labeled.
- **F06** Five preselected positions P22/P12/P21/P23/P32: complex G, G magnitude and phase, native raw PE/BIE magnitude, signed G dB and wrapped G phase residual. G is present only at P22/P21/P23; P12/P32 remain gaps. Native rough-field phase is not treated as a comparable cross-method residual. Inspection: five x-axis IDs remain, two missing G positions remain blank, and the raw-magnitude panel includes all five.
- **F07** Distinct estimands: original weighted M99 error versus local 3-point E_subset; line and local exact squared-error magnitude/phase contributions; local mean/median/max relative and absolute differences; full nine-point *raw-amplitude-only* summary. The full nine-point complex G `E_array` is explicitly unavailable. Inspection: fractions are labeled as algebraic squared-error terms, and the raw-amplitude panel does not imply phase comparability.

Source figure-data and a pure redraw entrypoint are `plot_data/plot_data.mat` and `scripts/reporting/render_two_dimensional_closeout_s0_s1.m`. `audit/redraw_comparison.csv` records pixel equality for all seven PNGs.
