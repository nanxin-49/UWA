## Interpretation

- The transmitted test signal is an analytic baseband LFM envelope; figures also show its real passband projection.
- The received LFM is generated by multiplying the LFM spectrum by `H_f` or `H_reflect_f` interpolated onto the LFM FFT grid.
- This validates receiver-side waveform and matched-filter statistics. It does not alter the PE/WAPE source field and does not call the communication script.
- Single-seed waveforms are not expected to match pointwise; ensemble energy, envelope, PDP, and matched-filter trends are the comparison targets.
- In this seed_count=32, Nf=32 run, total-channel metrics look much better than reflected-only metrics, so reflected LFM should be treated as the primary diagnostic for the two reflection branches.
