## Scope and reproduction level

This is a validation-only MATLAB implementation of the dimensionless Fredholm collocation method and the reflection-mode reconstruction in B. J. Kachoyan and C. Macaskill, JASA 82(5), 1720--1726 (1987), DOI 10.1121/1.395165. The paper does not publish the exact 256-point Gaussian or modified Pierson--Moskowitz arrays, random seeds, or all kernel implementation details needed for pixel-identical figures. The Gaussian and PM figures below therefore use fixed, auditable replacement realizations. Equation/discretization/metric checks are strict at the implemented formula level; figure-level numerical agreement is approximate and is not claimed as a successful pixel-level reproduction.

## Formula mapping

- Surface: `z=hhat*xi(x)` with `khat=kL`, `hhat=h/L`; incident angle is from the horizontal, `alpha0=cos(theta)`, `gamma0=sin(theta)`.
- Boundary forcing: psi_inc uses gamma0+alpha0*hhat*xi-prime and exp(i*khat*(alpha0*x-gamma0*hhat*xi)).
- Fredholm equation: (I+dx*K)*psi=2*psi_inc; Eq.(5) uses the H1_1 kernel, rho^2=(xprime-x)^2+hhat^2*(xiprime-xi)^2, and the curvature diagonal limit.
- Kernel implementation: K=-(i*pi/2)*khat*hhat^2*[xi_source-xi_target-dx*xi_prime_target]*H1_1(khat*rho)/rho; diagonal=0.5*hhat^2*xi_second/(1+hhat^2*xi_prime^2).
- Reflection modes: q_r=alpha0+2*pi*r/(khat*N*dx), gamma_r=sqrt(1-q_r^2); carrier-demodulated psi_hat and C use the paper DFT convention and only real propagating modes.
- Reflection coefficient evaluation: psi_hat uses carrier-demodulated ifft; C uses fft(exp(-i*khat*gamma*hhat*xi))/N; the circular lookup is C at r-minus-m.
- Energy: `E=Re(sum(abs(R_r).^2.*gamma_r/gamma0))`. Near-field reconstruction is only evaluated at `z>hhat*max(xi)` as required by Appendix A.
